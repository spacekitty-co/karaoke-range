# Karaoke Range privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Karaoke Range finds karaoke songs that suit your singing range. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

## The microphone

The app uses your phone's microphone only while you are measuring your range on the My range screen, doing the warm-up, playing the Echo game, the hold challenge or the Pop! balloon game, or singing along to a song, to work out which note you are singing. The sound is analysed on your phone, a fraction of a second at a time, and then discarded. It is never recorded to a file, never stored, and never uploaded.

You can turn off microphone access at any time in your phone's settings. The range finder, warm-up, Echo game, hold challenge, Pop! balloon game and sing-along need it; the rest of the app works without it.

## What is saved on your phone

- Your lowest and highest notes, and the date and result of each range measurement, for the history chart
- For each song you have sung along to, the lowest and highest notes you sang in it
- How long you sang today, and your streak of days on which you met the daily goal
- Your character's name, look and heart level
- When you last played with her or sang, so she can greet you according to how long you were away
- Your hearts to spend, and the room decorations you bought and placed
- Your wish list: the title and artist of songs you shared to the app (for example from Spotify) that are not in its song list yet
- Your sound setting and whether you have seen the welcome

These are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

## What is not collected

- The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
- The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
- The Android version of the app does not have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## Spotify links

Song cards have a play button that opens a Spotify search for that song, in the Spotify app or your web browser. Karaoke Range only passes the song title and artist to that search. What happens next is covered by Spotify's own privacy policy.

## Sharing a song to the app

You can share a song from Spotify or another app to Karaoke Range. The app receives only the text that app shares, usually the song title, the artist and a link. It reads the title and artist on your phone to find the song in its song list; it does not open the link. If you add the song to your wish list, its title and artist are saved as described above.

## Sending feedback

Settings has a Send feedback button. It opens your own email app with a new message addressed to the developer; nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. If you do, your email and its contents reach the developer by ordinary email, are used only to reply and to improve the app, and are not shared with anyone.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date, and shown in the app.
